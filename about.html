Our Story

A Team Built on Medical Expertise

Experienced Leadership Team
Founders & Managing Directors
25+ combined years in Nepal's medical & pharma sector

Central Surgical Concern Pvt. Ltd. was established in 2012 by a team of seasoned professionals who collectively bring over 25 years of experience from Nepal's leading pharmaceutical and healthcare companies including roles at Dabur Pharma, VijayaDeep Laboratories, and National Healthcare.

This hands on medical industry background means we don't just import equipment. We understand how it's used, what healthcare providers need, and how to ensure the right product reaches the right facility at the right time.

From government hospitals to private clinics, we serve the full spectrum of Nepal's healthcare sector, acting as a reliable bridge between world-class global manufacturers and local healthcare providers.

How We Got Here

1999–2012

Building Medical Industry Expertise

The founding team accumulated over 25 years of combined experience working across Nepal's pharmaceutical and medical device industry including roles in medical sales, divisional management, and business development.

Central Surgical Concern Founded

The company was established in Kathmandu with a clear mission: to become Nepal's most reliable importer of quality medical and surgical equipment from global manufacturers.
